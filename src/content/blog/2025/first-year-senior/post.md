The title changed on a Tuesday. The job took most of a year to change with it,
and nobody tells you which parts are supposed to change first.

The first thing I got wrong was assuming the promotion was about writing harder
code. It was not. The hard code was the part I was already good at; that is why
the title moved. The part I was bad at was everything that happens before
anyone writes code — deciding what not to build, saying the unpopular estimate
out loud, noticing that two people had quietly built the same thing twice.

The second thing I got wrong was reviewing pull requests like a compiler. A
review that only catches defects is a review a linter could have done. The
reviews people remembered were the ones where I asked why the change existed
at all, and about a third of the time the honest answer changed the change.

A year in, the work I am proudest of is a design doc that ended with us not
building anything.
