Left at 6am to beat the heat, which worked for exactly two hours. The plan: the
full Kelly Brook loop, counterclockwise this time, with the gravel cut-through
at mile 40 that I have been eyeing since March.

Counterclockwise turns out to be the right call. The long climb out of the
valley comes at mile 25 instead of mile 75, and the fast descent lands at the
point in the day where morale needs it most. The gravel section is two miles of
loose baby-head rock and I would do it again immediately.

Mile 80 is where centuries get honest. Everything above 80 is a negotiation
between your legs and the last store stop, and the negotiation goes better with
more water than I brought.

Notes for next time: two bottles at the store instead of one, start thirty
minutes earlier, and never trust a road named "Brook" to be flat.
