Mile 80 is where centuries get honest. Notes for next time: more water at the
store stop, and never trust a road named "Brook" to be flat.

## What the loop actually asks for

Three things, in the order they bite:

- Water. There is exactly one store between mile 52 and the finish.
- Gearing. The gap averages 7% for two miles, on tired legs, in the sun.
- Patience. Counterclockwise front-loads the climbing and saves the fast
  descent for when you have stopped enjoying descending.

Clockwise next time, probably. Ask me again in June.
